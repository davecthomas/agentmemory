## 2026-04-20T21:48Z · davecthomas · fix/deterministic-runtime-detection

**Decision:** Runtime detection resolves payload, then process ancestry, then env vars, and fails closed to an UnknownAdapter.
**Why:** Detection fell through to Codex whenever CLAUDECODE or GEMINI_CLI did not reach the hook subprocess, misattributing shards and spawning bootstraps that presumed Codex credentials in Claude-only repos.
**Alternatives:** Require client repos to export a discovery env var: rejected, detection must work without repo cooperation.
**Scope:** scripts/shared-repo-memory/adapters/
**Commit:** 0bce702

## 2026-04-20T22:38Z · davecthomas · feat/uninstaller

**Decision:** Uninstall is symmetric with install and removes only entries it can prove are its own.
**Why:** Installation was one-way; backing out meant hand-editing hook files and config, and risked removing user-added hooks for unrelated tools.
**Scope:** scripts/shared-repo-memory/uninstall.py
**Commit:** b8196fe
